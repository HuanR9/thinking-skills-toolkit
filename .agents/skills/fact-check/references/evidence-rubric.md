# Evidence rubric

## Claim categories

- **Fact:** observable or externally verifiable statement.
- **Inference:** conclusion drawn from facts.
- **Value judgment:** statement about what is desirable, fair, or important.

## Evidence checks

For every factual subclaim, inspect source authority, originalness, date, sample/denominator, definitions, missing context, conflicts, and independence of corroboration.

## Verdict scale

| Verdict | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Confirmed | Strong, direct, current evidence supports the claim as written. |
| Broadly supported but needs narrowing | Core is supported but scope, certainty, or wording is too broad. |
| Disputed | Credible evidence materially conflicts. |
| Insufficient evidence | Available evidence cannot support a reliable conclusion. |
| Clearly false | Strong evidence contradicts the claim as written. |

Do not convert absence of evidence into evidence of absence unless the search coverage justifies it.
