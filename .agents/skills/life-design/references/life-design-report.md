# Life design blueprint

1. **Current position:** balance scan and evidence, not a diagnosis.
2. **Designable problem:** reframe the initial concern; distinguish constraints from choices.
3. **Compass:** work view, life view, tensions, and what remains uncertain.
4. **Energy map:** flow, replenishment, depletion, and environment conditions.
5. **Three viable directions:** title, five-year sketch, questions to validate, resources, attraction, confidence, and alignment.
6. **Prototype action plan:** conversations, short experiences, reversible tests, and a first action this week.
7. **Non-negotiables and update rule:** boundaries the user chooses and how feedback will update the plan.

Describe all interpretations as provisional and based on the user's self-report.
